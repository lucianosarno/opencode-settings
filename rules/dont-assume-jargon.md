# Don't assume jargon familiarity

When you use a specialized term, help the reader on **first use in that section** with a short plain-language gloss **inside the sentence** (or right after an em dash / parentheses). User cognitive load should go to decisions, not decoding vocabulary.

## Do

- Prefer the common industry name; gloss once, then use the term normally.
- Example: “OAuth (credenciais de aplicativo que geram um token temporário)”
- Example: “Service Tag — o número de série Dell do equipamento”

## Don't

- Dump a long glossary at the end unless the document is long and many terms keep recurring.
- Explain everyday words, or re-gloss the same term every paragraph.
- Invent solemn labels or stack acronyms to sound expert when a normal phrase works.

Works together with `fluent-readable-prose`: clarity in flow, not terminology theater.
